# Rogue-Market-UI-UX-Prototype
A marketplace web application prototype designed to provide a simple, user-friendly platform for discovering, buying, and selling products online


🛍️ Rogue Market

Rogue Market is an online marketplace web application where users can buy and sell products. It is designed to give users a simple and convenient way to find products, interact with sellers, and make transactions.

What makes Rogue Market different is that it is not only focused on regular buying and selling. It also includes features such as bidding, trading, live selling, and seller stores to make the marketplace more interactive.

📌 About the Project

Rogue Market was created as a marketplace concept that combines common online shopping features with other ways of buying and selling.

Users can browse products, search for items, add products to their cart, buy products, join bidding activities, and explore trading options. Sellers can also create their own store and showcase their products.

The project focuses on creating an interface that is easy to understand, organized, and convenient to use for both buyers and sellers.

User Roles

Rogue Market has two main types of users:

Buyer

A buyer can:

- Browse products
- Search for products
- View product details
- Add products to the cart
- Buy products
- Choose an available payment method
- Join product bidding
- Explore trade options
- Interact with sellers
- Get help through customer service

Seller

A seller can:

- Create a seller account
- Set up their own store
- Add products
- Manage their product listings
- Sell products
- Do live selling
- Interact with buyers
- Manage their transactions

Main Features

Search Products

Users can search for products they are interested in and find items available in the marketplace.

Cart

Buyers can add products to their cart and review the items before buying.

Buy Now

The Buy Now option allows buyers to directly purchase a product.

Bidding

Buyers can join a bidding activity for products instead of only buying them at a fixed price.

Trade

Rogue Market also allows users to trade products, giving them another option aside from buying and selling.

Live Selling

Sellers can use live selling to showcase their products and interact with potential buyers.

Seller Store

Sellers can create their own store where they can display and sell their products.

Payment

Buyers can proceed with their purchase using the available payment options, including Cash on Delivery (COD).

Seller Verification

Sellers go through a verification process that includes submitting business-related requirements before using seller features.

Customer Service

The application also provides a customer service section where users can get assistance with their concerns.

UI/UX Design

The design of Rogue Market focuses on making the application simple, organized, and easy to navigate.

The interface was designed with the user in mind, especially when browsing products, buying items, selling products, and moving between different sections of the application.

Some of the main UI/UX considerations include:

- Easy navigation
- Clear buttons and actions
- Organized product information
- Simple user flow
- Consistent design
- User control
- Error prevention
- Easy-to-understand interface
